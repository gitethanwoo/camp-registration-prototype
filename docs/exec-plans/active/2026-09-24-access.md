## Purpose / Big Picture

WinShape collects health information about campers. For Day Camp and Family Camp the family fills in a health form inside this platform during registration (allergies, medications, physician, insurance). For Overnight Camp the health profile lives in CampDoc, a separate system, and this platform only knows whether the family finished it. Today the platform stores the embedded health forms (the `HealthJson` column on `Registrations`) but nobody can read them, nobody can choose how a program collects health information except by editing the whole program, and nothing controls which staff may see those details. The staff roster itself exists only inside WorkOS (the identity provider; in production this is Microsoft Entra), so an admin cannot see who has access, what they can see, or when they last signed in.

After this plan, Alex Morgan (admin persona, `alex.morgan@winshape.example`) can:

- K11, Staff access (`/admin/setup/users`): see every staff member synced from the WorkOS organization `org_winshape_staff`, with their role, ministry scope, health-data access, last sign-in and status. People removed from the organization in WorkOS show as Revoked on the next sync, which runs automatically when the page opens and the last sync is more than 15 minutes old, or on "Sync now". Alex can change a person's ministry scope and health-data access; both changes are audited with before and after values. There is no password field, no password reset and no "add user" form: people are added and removed in the identity provider.
- K9, Health collection settings (`/admin/setup/health`): for each program, choose the collection mechanism (Embedded form, Third-party form with its link, or CampDoc), with a warning and an explicit confirmation when CampDoc is chosen for anything other than Overnight Camp, and choose which staff roles may view health details. The page lists the people who can actually view details right now.

And the settings are enforced: a staff member can open a camper's embedded health form (the "View health form" button on the admin registration detail page) only if they are active, have health-data access, hold a role the program allows, and their ministry scope covers the program. Every successful view writes a `health.viewed` audit row, and every refusal writes `health.view_denied`, both visible in the audit log (K12) and on the registration's Activity tab.

To see it working: start the API and Vite (Concrete Steps), sign in as Diane (CET), open a Day Camp registration and click "View health form": she is refused because she has no health-data access. Sign in as Alex, open Users, give Diane "Health details", open Health settings, allow the CET role on Day Camp · Atlanta and save. Diane tries again and sees the allergies and medications; the registration's Activity tab shows the view.

## Context and Orientation

The repository is a .NET 10 minimal API (`api/Camp.Api`) over SQL Server with EF Core, and a Vue 3 web app (`web/`) built from shadcn-vue primitives in `web/src/components/ui`. Feature slices plug in through seams so they rarely edit shared files: an `IEndpointModule` class maps routes (found by assembly scan), an `IEntityTypeConfiguration<T>` maps an entity, an `ISeedModule` inserts demo rows at startup, and `web/src/features/<slice>/routes.ts` exports `adminRoutes` whose `meta.nav` adds a sidebar entry. All of this is described in `AGENTS.md` and `web/src/features/README.md`.

Identity: staff sign in through WorkOS AuthKit (locally the WorkOS emulator on port 4100, configured in `infra/workos/workos-emulate.config.yaml`). `api/Camp.Api/Auth/AuthEndpoints.cs` redeems the code, reads the organization and role, and issues the app's own cookie with a `camp:staff_role` claim. `Auth/Identity.cs` defines the policies (`Policies.Admin`, `Policies.Staff` and others) and the `StaffUser` record endpoints inject. Tests sign in with `factory.SignInAsStaff("admin")`, which posts to a Testing-only `/api/auth/dev-login` that goes through the same `SignInAsync`.

Health data today: `CampProgram.HealthMechanism` (`Embedded`, `ThirdParty`, `CampDoc`) in `Domain/Entities.cs`; `Registration.HealthStatus` (Complete, Incomplete, Missing, Not required) and `Registration.HealthJson`, the serialized `HealthForm` record from `Features/Checkout.cs` (Dietary, Allergies, AdaNeeds, Medications, PhysicianName, PhysicianPhone, InsuranceProvider). `Features/AdminEndpoints.cs` returns `HealthOnFile` but never the contents.

Audit: inject `IAuditLog` and call `Record(action, entityType, entityId, detail)` before `SaveChanges`. The setup slice's `SetupAudit.Record(audit, db, …, changes)` extension (in `Features/Setup/SetupShared.cs`) also writes before/after rows that the K12 audit log shows; this slice uses it.

Terms: "ministry scope" is the one ministry (for example WSC Camps) a staff member works in, or All ministries. "Health-data access" is a per-person flag: on means the person may view health details where the program allows their role; off means they see completion status only. "Sync" means reading the WorkOS organization's memberships and reconciling the platform's staff rows with them.

## Plan of Work

API, in `api/Camp.Api/Features/Access/`:

`AccessEntities.cs` defines `StaffMember` (WorkOS user id, email, first and last name, role, `MinistryId` null for all, `HealthAccess`, `Status` Active or Revoked, `LastSignInAt`, `SyncedAt`, `RevokedAt`, `CreatedAt`), `StaffSyncRun` (when, who, added, updated, revoked counts) and `ProgramHealthSetting` (program id, allowed viewer roles as a comma list, third-party form URL, updated at and by), with their `IEntityTypeConfiguration`s (unique email, unique program id).

`StaffDirectory.cs` reads the WorkOS memberships through a named `HttpClient` ("workos-directory") created from `IHttpClientFactory`, following `list_metadata.after` for paging. `StaffSync.cs` reconciles: add new members, update names and roles (auditing role changes), restore a revoked member who is back, revoke active rows that are no longer members. `StaffAccessRules.cs` holds the four-check health rule and the effective viewer list.

`AccessEndpoints.cs` maps, all under `/api/access` and `Policies.Admin` unless noted: `GET staff`, `POST staff/sync`, `GET staff/{id}`, `PUT staff/{id}` (ministry scope and health access; 409 when revoked); `GET health`, `PUT health/{programId}`; and `GET registrations/{id}/health` under `Policies.Staff`.

`AccessSeed.cs` (Order 950) seeds staff rows for the emulator personas and Morgan Ellis, a health setting per program (admin only for embedded and third-party programs, none for CampDoc), and the Day Camp `HealthJson` backfill.

## Idempotence and Recovery

The seed checks for its own rows (by email, by program id, and only fills a null `HealthJson`), so restarting the API is safe. Sync is idempotent: running it twice with no WorkOS change adds, updates and revokes nothing. To reset the slice database, drop `CampRegistration_Access` (see the environment notes in the master plan) and restart the API.
