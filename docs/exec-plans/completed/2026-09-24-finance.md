## Purpose / Big Picture

WinShape's finance team (persona Marcus Reed, WorkOS role `finance`) has nowhere in the staff console to see money. After this change Marcus can open a Finance group in the staff sidebar and:

- read registrations, settled revenue, attendance and demographics for any ministry, program, session and date range, with a "Certified metric" badge on the governed measures and a CSV export, where the revenue figure ties to the reconciliation screen to the cent (screen FN1);
- compare a Fiserv settlement batch with the payments the platform recorded, see matched, unmatched and fee lines, open an unmatched line and resolve it (match it to a registration or book it as an adjustment, with an audited note), and see the Oracle Fusion journal status of each batch (FN2);
- work the list of failed payment-plan installments: when the next automatic retry is, when the grace period ends, how many days are left before the cancellation policy applies, and act with "Retry now" (charges the card on file through the fake Fiserv gateway) or "Contact family" (queues a HubSpot email through the outbox) (FN3);
- see every Oracle Fusion journal batch with its date, entry count, totals and status (Posted, Failed, Pending), read the error on a failed one, and resubmit it (FN4).

A family (persona Maria Johnson) can apply for financial assistance for an upcoming registration, with household details pre-filled and a supporting document uploaded, and follow the application's status (O6). Staff review the queue, read the application and the document, and approve an award or deny it. The server refuses any award where scholarships plus discounts would exceed 100% of the price, or that is more than the family still owes. An approved award lowers the order's balance, which every family and staff page reads (O7).

To see it working: start the API and web app (Concrete Steps), sign in as Maria, open the account menu, choose "Scholarships", and apply for Family Camp. Sign in as Marcus, open Operations → Scholarships, approve an award, then walk Finance → Reports, Reconciliation, Plan exceptions and Oracle exports.

## Surprises & Discoveries

- EF Core can't translate a query-syntax left join from a nullable foreign key (`SettlementLine.PaymentOperationId`) through to the order's navigations, even with `(int?)` casts on both keys. Evidence: `InvalidOperationException: The LINQ expression 'DbSet<SettlementLine>().LeftJoin(...)' could not be translated`. `Ledger.Lines` now loads lines with `PaymentOperation.OrderId`, then the orders by id, and joins in memory.

- Observation: the family slice's e2e spec makes exact assertions about Maria's F1 checklist and F4 lists (for example one `#checklist li` containing "Payment plan", "Past (1)", one "View details for Day Camp" link). ## Context and Orientation

The repository is a WinShape camp registration prototype: an ASP.NET Core minimal API in `api/Camp.Api` (EF Core on SQL Server), a Vue 3 app with shadcn-vue components in `web/`, and Playwright specs in `e2e/`. `AGENTS.md` explains the slice seams: a slice adds `api/Camp.Api/Features/<Slice>/` (endpoint modules implementing `IEndpointModule`, EF mappings implementing `IEntityTypeConfiguration<T>`, a seed implementing `ISeedModule`), `web/src/features/<slice>/routes.ts` (guest `routes` and staff `adminRoutes`, where `meta.nav` adds a sidebar entry), `api/Camp.Api.Tests/<Slice>Tests.cs`, and `e2e/<slice>.spec.ts`. This slice is `Finance` / `finance`.

Money is stored in cents everywhere. The core entities are in `api/Camp.Api/Domain/Entities.cs`: a `PaymentOrder` (one checkout, with a confirmation code such as `WS-7A3L0E`) holds `Registration`s (one per camper, each with `PriceCents`, `DiscountCents`, `PaidCents`, and a computed `BalanceCents`), `PaymentOperation`s (Charge, Authorize, Refund, Void, each with a processor reference like `fsv_…`), and `Installment`s (a payment plan's scheduled charges, Scheduled/Paid/Failed). `IPaymentGateway` in `api/Camp.Api/Integrations/PaymentGateway.cs` is the port to Fiserv; `FakeFiservGateway` approves card 4242 4242 4242 4242 and declines 4000 0000 0000 0002. The family slice's `BalancePaymentService` (`api/Camp.Api/Features/Family/BalancePaymentService.cs`) charges a balance or retries a failed installment safely. The outbox (`OutboxEvent`) is how emails reach HubSpot; `IAuditLog.Record` stages an audit row that the caller's `SaveChanges` commits.

Terms used here: a "settlement batch" is the file Fiserv sends each day listing the card transactions it paid out, with its fees; "reconciliation" is matching each line of that file to a payment the platform recorded; a "journal batch" is the accounting entry WinShape posts to Oracle Fusion (its general ledger) for one settlement batch; "grace period" is the 7 days after a failed installment during which the family can still pay (Program Policies: retries after 3 and 7 days).
